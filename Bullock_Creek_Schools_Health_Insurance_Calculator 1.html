Methodology & notes
• Employee annual costs are the caps-adjusted annual employee contributions from the Bullock Creek Schools quote.
• Plans: BCBSM Enhanced 1000 173 and Enhanced HSA 2000 121/122 (BCBSM Benefits-at-a-Glance, effective 1/1/2025); BCBSM Enhanced HSA Level 126/127 (Benefits-at-a-Glance, effective 1/1/2026); Priority Health HSA 2500 PH01 (Schedule of Benefits, effective 1/1/2026).
• Medical costs assume one $150 office visit per $500 of medical cost (up to 12 visits). On Enhanced 1000 173, office visits are a $20 copay not subject to the deductible; all other medical costs go to the deductible, then are covered 100%. On the HSA plans, all medical costs apply to the deductible first, then are covered 100%.
• Rx is modeled at the per-fill maximum: generic $10; preferred brand/specialty $80; non-preferred brand/specialty $100 on BCBSM plans and $160 on Priority Health PH01. On HSA plans, before the deductible is met each fill costs 3x that amount (applied to the deductible); after the deductible, the regular amount applies. Enhanced 1000 173 Rx is not subject to the deductible.
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage. Rx counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum.
• Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included. Estimates are for illustration only; actual costs depend on claims and the plan documents.
